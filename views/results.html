Evaluation guide

Metric Definitions

Avg Quiz Score

The average number of correct quiz answers achieved after watching a video or summary.

Avg Difference Score

The average difference between a participant's summary quiz score and their original-video quiz score within the same form. A value near 0 means the summary performed close to the original-video baseline.

Avg Completeness

The average participant rating for how much important content from the original tutorial was preserved in the summary.

Avg Clarity

The average participant rating for how understandable and easy to follow the summary was.

Avg Coherence

The average participant rating for how logically organized and connected the summary was.
